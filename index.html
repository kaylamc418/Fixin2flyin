01

Mobile Bike Repair

02

Trail Preparation

Pre-ride safety checks and practical preparation for local rides, mountain trips, trail days, and longer adventures.

03

Flat Repair

Bike Coaching

Dom + Lumi

The rider, the builder, and the trail companion.

Fixin’ 2 Flyin’ is built around Dom’s experience riding, repairing bikes, preparing for the trail, and helping other riders move forward with greater confidence. His approach is practical, personal, and grounded in real riding experience.

Lumi is part of the journey too—showing up in the trails, basecamps, quiet moments, and adventures that make Fixin’ 2 Flyin’ about more than the bike itself.

Together, Dom and Lumi represent the personal side of the brand: movement, loyalty, exploration, and the freedom found outdoors.

For Dom’s Mama

For the woman who helped shape the ride.

Fixin’ 2 Flyin’ carries more than a love of bikes, trails, and adventure. It also carries the strength, encouragement, and lasting influence of Dom’s mama.

Her support helped shape the person Dom became—the determination to keep moving forward, the courage to take on difficult climbs, and the heart to help others along the way. Those lessons remain part of every repair, every ride, and every rider Dom encourages.

This website is also a place to honor her life, her love, and the impact she continues to have. Her memory is carried forward in the work, the adventures, and the moments that make the journey meaningful.

Always remembered. Always part of the ride.
