Services

Repair the bike. Build the rider.

Fixin’ 2 Flyin’ combines convenient mobile bicycle service with practical mountain bike coaching. Dom provides on-site repair, tune-ups, brake and gear adjustments, trail preparation, and personalized riding guidance without requiring riders to transport their bikes to a traditional shop.

Mobile Bike Repair

On-site tune-ups, adjustments, basic maintenance, and ride-readiness checks.

Trail Prep

Pre-ride inspections for mountain routes, trail days, longer rides, and first rides back.

Flat Repair

Tube and tire support, pressure checks, and simple troubleshooting before the next ride.

Brake + Gear Adjustments

Brake feel, shifting performance, cable tension, and control-point checks.

Bike Coaching

One-on-one help for body position, braking, climbing, cornering, handling, jumping, and control.

Ride Support

Ride planning, gear checks, route prep, confidence, and personal progression goals.

Why Choose Dom

Why Choose Dom

Mobile support, trail knowledge, and coaching that stays grounded in what riders actually need to progress.

01

Mobile convenience

Repairs and adjustments come directly to the rider, reducing the time and difficulty involved in transporting a bicycle to a shop.

02

Repair and coaching in one service

Dom can address both the bike and the rider, helping improve mechanical readiness, riding control, confidence, and trail preparation.

03

Individual attention

One-on-one guidance allows the session to focus on the rider’s current experience, goals, and areas for improvement.

04

Real-world trail focus

Coaching can cover practical foundations such as body position, braking, cornering, line choice, momentum, and controlled progression.
