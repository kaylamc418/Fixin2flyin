Dom Code

Fix with precision. Ride with purpose.

Every part of Fixin 2 Flyin reflects Dom’s range: the practical skill to dial in the bike, the experience to coach the rider, and the confidence to approach every line with control and intention.

  1. 01

    BIKE READY

    Dial It

    Tire pressure, brakes, shifting, safety checks, adjustments, and the small details that make a bike feel ride-ready.

  2. 02

    TRAIL AWARE

    Read It

    Line choice, trail conditions, body position, braking points, speed, and control.

  3. 03

    CONTROL FIRST

    Commit

    Build confidence without sacrificing judgment. Stronger riding comes from sharper skills and smarter decisions.

  4. 04

    PROGRESSION UNLOCKED

    Fly

    More skill, more trust, and a bike prepared to keep up with the rider’s next goal.
